      text: |-
        My research combines grey systems theory with data-driven modelling to support forecasting and decision-making under limited information. I am particularly interested in seasonal time series, deteriorating inventory, and the dynamics underlying forecasting models.

        Applications of my work include traffic speed forecasting, air quality prediction, inventory management, and the estimation of house price trajectories from sparse transaction data.
